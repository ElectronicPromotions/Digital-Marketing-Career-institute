Learning outcomes.

Every module is built around measurable outcomes. On completing the programme you will be able to:

  • Analyse the non-linear buyer journey and map the decision touchpoints that move a customer from awareness to action
  • Construct a Unique Value Proposition and data-backed buyer personas using psychographic and AI-assisted segmentation
  • Set SMART goals and evaluate performance with LTV:CAC, engagement scoring and ROAS rather than vanity metrics
  • Design a lean, agile strategy with the Lean Canvas, growth loops and rapid test-and-learn sprints
  • Plan and optimise paid media across funnel stages using PPC, CPM and retail media models
  • Implement GA4 event tracking and server-side tagging, and apply predictive and prescriptive analytics
  • Build an owned-media engine from first-party data, lead magnets and automated drip sequences
  • Monitor core signals and detect anomalies before they cost revenue
